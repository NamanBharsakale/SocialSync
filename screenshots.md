# SocialSync — Screenshots

A visual walkthrough of the SocialSync platform, covering all major pages and features.

---

## 1. Hero Section (Landing Page)

> The landing page introduces SocialSync with a bold headline, feature highlights, and a clear call-to-action to get started. The hero section communicates the core value proposition: AI-powered social media automation that saves time and drives engagement.

![Hero Section](screenshots/hero-section.png)

---

## 2. Login & Sign Up Page

> Users can securely sign in to their existing account or register a new one from the same page. The auth page supports toggling between Login and Sign Up views, with real-time validation and helpful error messages.

![Login and Sign Up Page](screenshots/login-signup.png)

---

## 3. Dashboard

> The dashboard gives users a bird's-eye view of their social media activity — connected accounts, recent posts, upcoming scheduled content, and quick-access actions for composing or scheduling a new post.

![Dashboard](screenshots/dashboard.png)

---

## 4. AI Composer

> The AI Composer lets users generate high-quality social media posts by providing a prompt and selecting a tone (casual, professional, inspirational, etc.). The AI returns ready-to-use content along with an optional AI-generated image, which the user can edit before saving or scheduling.

![AI Composer](screenshots/ai-composer.png)

---

## 5. Scheduler

> The Scheduler page allows users to write or paste post content, attach media, choose target platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter), and pick an exact date and time for publishing. Posts can be saved as drafts or queued for automatic publishing.

![Scheduler](screenshots/scheduler.png)

---

## 6. Scheduled Posts

> The Scheduled Posts view lists all upcoming and past posts with their status (scheduled, published, failed). Users can see which platforms each post is targeting, the scheduled date and time, and a preview of the content and attached media.

![Scheduled Posts](screenshots/scheduled-posts.png)

---

## 7. LinkedIn — Published Post

> A real LinkedIn post published automatically by SocialSync via the scheduler. This screenshot demonstrates a live post on the user's LinkedIn profile, confirming end-to-end automated publishing without any manual action from the user.

![LinkedIn Published Post](screenshots/linkedin-published-post.png)

---

## 8. Connected Accounts

> The Accounts page shows all social media profiles connected through Zernio OAuth. Users can see account status, sync connections, and add or remove platforms. A manual Sync button refreshes the account list from Zernio in real time.

![Connected Accounts](screenshots/connected-accounts.png)

---

## 9. Reset Link (Forgot Password Email)

> When a user requests a password reset, SocialSync sends a secure, time-limited email containing a reset link. The email is delivered via Gmail SMTP and the link expires after 1 hour for security.

![Password Reset Email](screenshots/reset-link-email.png)
